# Thermal Management and Battery Cooling Architectures

## Cooling Architectures Comparison
1. **Air Cooling (Passive and Forced):**
   - Air is blown across cell cylindrical or prismatic surfaces.
   - Low cost and complexity, but limited heat transfer coefficient (10–50 W/m^2*K); prone to thermal gradients between upstream and downstream cells.
2. **Indirect Liquid Cooling (Cold Plates):**
   - Ethylene glycol / water mixture circulates through aluminum cold plates clamped to cell bases or sides.
   - High heat transfer coefficient (200–1000 W/m^2*K); capable of handling sustained fast charging loads.
3. **Direct Dielectric Immersion Cooling:**
   - Cells are submerged directly in electrically non-conductive dielectric hydrocarbon or synthetic fluorochemical fluids.
   - Eliminates thermal interfaces; maximum cooling uniformity; prevents cell-to-cell thermal runaway propagation.

## Thermal Interface Materials (TIM)
High-performance thermal pads, gap fillers, or silicone adhesives (conductivity 2–6 W/m*K) are required to bridge micro-gaps between cell casings and cold plates, eliminating insulating air pockets.

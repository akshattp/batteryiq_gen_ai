# Battery Pack Inspection and Maintenance Checklist

## Field Inspection Workflow
Before returning any battery pack to operational status after an alert, maintenance engineers must follow a structured physical inspection protocol:

## Phase 1: Visual and Mechanical Check
1. **Enclosure Integrity:** Inspect the outer battery casing for bulging, mechanical dents, breach of IP rating seals, or thermal discoloration.
2. **Busbar and Terminal Torque:** Verify busbar bolt torque against specification (typically 6–9 Nm for M6 terminals). Check for oxidation or contact fretting.
3. **Venting Discs:** Confirm pressure relief burst discs are intact and show no signs of electrolyte residue or rupture.

## Phase 2: Electrical and Insulation Measurements
1. **Insulation Resistance (Megohmmeter):** Measure DC insulation resistance between high-voltage positive/negative terminals and chassis ground (> 500 Ohm/V required).
2. **Open Circuit Voltage Uniformity:** Measure individual cell/module OCVs with an external calibrated 6.5-digit multimeter. Maximum delta-V between cells must not exceed 20 mV.
3. **Ground Fault Monitoring:** Verify isolation monitoring circuit operation.
